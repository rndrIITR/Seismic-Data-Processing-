What are these?

Δt is the sampling interval of the record. If the file contains a time column or a DT= / Time Step: header the value is filled in automatically. Units tells the app how to convert the numbers to m/s²; use scale factor for any extra multiplier (instrument constant, unit conversion, amplitude scaling).

2 · Baseline correction

Which method should I use?

Acceleration fit removes a least-squares polynomial directly from the accelerogram (the classic SeismoSignal-style correction). Velocity fit integrates first, fits the polynomial to the velocity trace, differentiates it and removes that trend from the acceleration – this is the PEER-style workflow and is usually better at killing displacement drift. A pre-event window (if the record has one) removes the instrument offset before anything else.

3 · Filter

Causal or acausal?

Causal (single forward pass) is what most record-processing agencies and SeismoSignal use; it distorts phase near the corners. Acausal filters forward and backward so the phase is exactly zero, at the price of a non-physical precursor before the P-arrival and a squared amplitude response (−6 dB at the corner instead of −3 dB).
