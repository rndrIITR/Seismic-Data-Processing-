Methodology & references

Show

Gutenberg & Richter (1944); Aki (1965); Utsu (1965); Weichert (1980); Stepp (1972); Shi & Bolt (1982); Gardner & Knopoff (1974). HMTK/OpenQuake currently implements GardnerKnopoffType1 with a selectable distance/time window model and Stepp1971/1972 plus Weichert occurrence methods. The Gardner–Knopoff window used here follows HMTK's published coefficients.

HMTK-specific: Gardner–Knopoff space window = 10^(0.1238M + 0.983) km; time window = 10^(0.5409M − 0.547) days for M<6.5 and 10^(0.032M + 2.7389) days for M≥6.5.
